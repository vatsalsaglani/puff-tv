Bring Puff home.

Puff Installer puts Puff on your Android TV, without terminal commands. Download the version for your Mac or Windows computer. Extract the zip, then open the helper.

Prepare once.

Read the Android SDK license and accept it if you agree. Choose Download Android tools. The helper gets the tools directly from Google.

One network. Two screens.

Put your computer and TV on the same trusted network. Enable the TV’s supported network debugging option. USB debugging alone may not be enough.

Find your TV.

Choose Find my TV, then select your TV from the results. If discovery misses it, enter its local address. TVs with pairing codes have a separate pairing form.

Look up at your TV.

Approve the debugging request on the TV with your remote. The installer checks that the device is an Android TV running Android nine or newer.

One last click.

Check the TV model, then choose Install Puff on this TV. The helper downloads Puff, checks the file, and installs it. Keep your TV on until it finishes.

A little more room.

Puff opens on the TV. Set up casting, Home controls, or optional cleanup there. This is a beta: discovery depends on your TV. The website has a USB drive fallback.
